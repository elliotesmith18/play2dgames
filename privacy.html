Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 October 2026

The short version: no accounts, no names, no emails. Your game progress stays in your browser. When you finish a daily puzzle, an anonymous score is saved so everyone can see how they compare. There are no ads or tracking right now, and if that changes they only switch on if you say yes.

Who we are

Play 2D Games (play2dgames.com) makes small browser games, including Relic. Questions about this policy or your data: hello@play2dgames.com.

What we collect and why

The player ID is a random code made in your browser. It isn't linked to your name, email or any account, and we don't try to identify who you are.

Cookies and similar storage

  • Essential: local storage for game progress and the site_consent cookie. These are needed for the games to work, so they don't need your consent.
  • Advertising and statistics: none are used today. If we add them, they'll only load after you choose "Accept all", and this policy will list them first.

Who else handles data

  • GitHub Pages hosts the website files. See the GitHub privacy statement.
  • Supabase stores the anonymous scores. See the Supabase privacy policy.

Fonts, code libraries and images are served from our own site, not from third-party networks. The artifact photos come from The Metropolitan Museum of Art's Open Access collection (public domain).

These providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under their own safeguards.

How long we keep it

Scores are kept so daily statistics stay accurate, and may be deleted or summarised later. Data in your browser stays until you clear it. Your cookie choice expires after 180 days.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or California law), you can ask to see, correct or delete data about you, or object to how it's used. Because scores are anonymous, we may need your help to find yours: tell us the date, puzzle and score. You can also clear everything on your side by clearing this site's data in your browser. Email hello@play2dgames.com, and you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Our games are for a general audience and don't ask for personal information. If you think a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change how we handle data, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Big changes, like adding ads, will come with a new cookie question.
